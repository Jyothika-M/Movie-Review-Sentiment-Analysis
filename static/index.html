🎥 Behind the Scenes

About the Model

DistilBERT

CineSense uses a fine-tuned DistilBERT model — a lighter, faster distillation of Google's BERT transformer. DistilBERT retains 97% of BERT's language understanding while being 40% smaller and 60% faster, making it ideal for real-time inference.

IMDb Dataset

The model was fine-tuned on the Large Movie Review Dataset: 50,000 highly polarised IMDb reviews split equally into 25,000 training and 25,000 test samples, balanced between positive and negative sentiment labels.

Fine-Tuning / Transfer Learning

Transfer learning allows us to take a model pre-trained on billions of words and adapt it to our specific task with a fraction of the data and compute. The base DistilBERT weights were frozen, and a binary classification head was trained for 3 epochs on the IMDb corpus, achieving over 93% accuracy on the held-out test set.
